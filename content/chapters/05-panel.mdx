Rev D, the version of our design that we have signed, has **{N.panes_per_hex.display}** panes of **{N.l7_thickness.display}** glass in each hexagon, and the drawings and the 3D model show it. On 23 October we decided that Rev E, the next version, will have **{N.reve_panes_per_hex.display}** smaller panes, **{N.reve_pane_side.display}** on a side. Its drawings come out on {N.drawing_release.display}.

Air pressure pulls on each seam with **{N.seam_tension.display}**. A solid block at each corner, the corner node, and a pinned fitting halfway along the seam, the hardpoint, carry that pull. Both are still proposals, sized in our computer models on 16 October. The **{N.bolts_per_seam.display}** bolts along a seam only press the seals shut, and the seals are **{N.reve_seal_rings.display}** butyl loops and a silicone bead.
