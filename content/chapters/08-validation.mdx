Every test runs on the computer first. Nothing is machined until its digital twin, the computer model of that test, has a signed test sheet. Before a test, the model writes down what every gauge should read. The test passes when the measurement comes within **{N.test_pass_tolerance.display}** of that prediction. If it misses, we change the model, never the data.
