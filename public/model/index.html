The two pods follow Marina's 13 October working images, sized from her April numbers (2.5 m and 5.5 m on a 10 m sphere) scaled to the 9.40 m envelope; their exact shape and mounts wait on her answers. The column, greenhouses, ropes and people are placeholders. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Walk inside

Float from stop to stop. Drag to look around; tap a glowing dot to move there.
With a Quest or Vision Pro, the VR button appears here and puts you inside at the current stop. The ropes and placeholder people are hidden in this mode.

Systems X-ray

The shell fades to a ghost and each system lights up, flowing the way it runs. Tap a label for what it is and where the number comes from.
Solar arrays on the pentagons feed a power bus in the column; the mothership adds power while docked. Air is cleaned behind a service pentagon and blown down the column; water condenses out of the air, goes through the filter in the column and out to the greenhouses.
