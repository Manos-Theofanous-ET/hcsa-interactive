## Depth convention (read this first)

Units mm. **z is panel depth**, square to the panel: z 0 the outer (space) face of the rib and frame grid, z 180 the pane seat plane, z 400 the cabin face of the main rib and the corner block. The 180 mm frame depth is panel depth z (z 0 to z 180, the same as the straight secondary ribs), not length along the leaning joint face. The joint face of every seam lies on the radial plane through the shell edge and the sphere centre; seen from a panel it leans toward the panel interior with depth, 20.905 deg from the panel normal on a hexagon and 16.472 deg on a pentagon. **z' is depth along that leaning face** from the shell edge (z = z' cos(lean) on the face). The frame's joint face is therefore 180 / cos(lean) long: 192.7 mm on a hexagon, 187.7 mm on a pentagon. Features that belong to the seam are placed along the joint face by z' and cut normal to it: glands at z' 18 and 32, RTV recess z' 42, seam bolt row z' 58, hardpoint fitting envelope z' 46-106, corner tie rows z' 133.8 and 380.0, dowel z' 256.9. At a corner the two joint planes meet on the **vertex line** (vertex to sphere centre), which leans 23.80 deg (hexagon) / 20.08 deg (pentagon) along the corner bisector in the panel frame and 11.64 deg off square to an edge in the joint face (0.2060 mm along the edge per mm of z'). Parts still drawn straight (z = z'): P-06, HP-01, HP-01M, PL-01, A-04. HP-02A, HP-02B, HP-03 and A-05 are drawn in the joint frame (X along the seam, Y normal to the joint plane, Z = z').

## Clashes and departures from the brief (for the review)

1. **CB-01 / CB-02 tie columns.** Columns at 130 and 186 mm (pitch 56) measured along the edge **from the vertex line at each row's depth**, so the columns run parallel to the vertex line; from the z 0 vertex the holes are at 157.6 / 208.3 mm (column 1, rows 1 / 2) and 213.6 / 264.3 mm (column 2); the dowels are in the same columns. What the true geometry allows: the landing's wall meets the leg's inner face 90.48 mm (hexagon) / 102.82 mm (pentagon) from the vertex line; a 23.5 hole clears it from 102.47 / 114.82, a 45 mm washer from 113.45 / 125.79, and clear of the 2 mm chamfer from 115.49 / 127.83. So the briefed 87 / 143 is not possible; the pentagon governs at 127.83; 130 is drawn (2 mm of flat seat beyond the chamfer on the pentagon), 6 mm nearer the vertex line than the 136 of 8 Nov, the same on both blocks. Columns square to the edge instead (fixed distance from the z 0 vertex) do not fit: at the z' 380 row the landing's edge is already 181 mm from the z 0 vertex on the pentagon. The C1 model should be rerun at these positions.
2. **CB-01 / CB-02 shape and landing.** Each block is one outline swept along the vertex line (an oblique prism) and cut flat to the panel planes z 0 and z 400: joint faces on the radial planes, leg inner faces parallel to them 50 mm away (measured normal; flat nut seats), leg ends and the landing all parallel to the vertex line. Legs are 230 from the vertex line at every depth. The landing is 110 wide with its bottom 57 mm (hexagon) / 62 mm (pentagon) from the vertex line (2 mm short of the re-entrant corner, and 20 mm of wall where that is possible), walls 36.6 / 42.4 mm long; wall to the joint face 20.424 / 17.099 mm (pentagon below 20: for Marco). Departure: the landing leans with the vertex line, it is not straight along z. A landing straight along z would break out through the joint faces 57.2 mm (hexagon) / 60.3 mm (pentagon) below the outer face, because the vertex line moves 176 / 146 mm along the bisector over the 400 depth. Consequence: the main rib's end has to be cut on that lean (23.80 / 20.08 deg from square); P-04 is still drawn square-ended. Machining: every face is a plane or an R10 cylinder parallel to the vertex line, no undercut. The two R10 landing corners are 437 mm (hexagon) / 426 mm (pentagon) long, too deep for an end mill plunging along the vertex line; they are cut from the open side with a 20 mm ball or R10 bull-nose cutter run along the line. With the vertex line on a rotary axis one set-up does the joint faces, inner faces, landing and all holes; the z 0 and z 400 end cuts take one more set-up each. Acute 69.1 deg (hexagon) / 73.5 deg (pentagon) edges at the end cuts, chamfered. The three chamfered spines leave a channel about 2 mm across down the vertex line (corner seal O10, not modelled).
3. **Rows.** Tie rows at z' 133.8 and 380.0 and dowels at z' 256.9 on both blocks (z 125.0, 355.0, 240.0 on the hexagon's face; 128.3, 364.4, 246.4 on the pentagon's). The hexagon's joint face is 428.2 mm long down the lean and the pentagon's 417.1, so on a hexagon-pentagon seam the two cabin ends are 11.1 mm apart along the face.

The 8 Nov solid had put the two plain bolts inside the block at y +-88.75, where the pad (y +-100, z 46-106) covered them, and then moved them deeper to z 118; that was an artefact of the solid.
